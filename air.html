Air · Scenic Flights · Aerial Wonders

Views the Earth keeps for those who fly

🇹🇷

Cappadocia Balloon Flight

Göreme, Turkey

Sunrise over a lunar landscape of fairy chimneys — the world's finest balloon flight.

  • 100+ balloons rising together at dawn
  • Rock-carved churches and underground cities below
  • Champagne landing in a vineyard after 1hr in the air

Best: Apr – Jun, Sep – Nov

🇺🇸

Grand Canyon Helicopter

Arizona, USA

Descend 1,500m below the rim — the canyon only truly reveals itself from above.

  • West Rim, South Rim & North Rim routes
  • Land on the canyon floor beside the Colorado River
  • Dawn flights when the light turns the rock to fire

Best: Mar – May, Sep – Nov

🇹🇿

Serengeti Balloon Safari

Tanzania

Float above the Great Migration — millions of wildebeest from 300 metres up.

  • Lions, elephants and cheetah from the air at dawn
  • Mara River crossing visible from above
  • Full bush breakfast on landing in the plains

Best: Jul – Oct (migration peak)

🇳🇿

Milford Sound Scenic Flight

Fiordland, New Zealand

Fly over the eighth wonder — glaciers, waterfalls and black fjord from a floatplane.

  • Mitre Peak — 1,692m straight out of the sea
  • Doubtful Sound and Dusky Sound from above
  • Glacier neve landings by ski-plane

Best: Oct – Apr

🇳🇦

Namib Desert Aerial

Namibia

The world's oldest desert from above — star dunes 300m high meeting the Atlantic.

  • Sossusvlei dunes — apricot and ochre from the air
  • Skeleton Coast — shipwrecks in the sand from 500m
  • Fish River Canyon — Africa's Grand Canyon

Best: May – Sep

🇨🇭

Swiss Alps Glacier Flight

Switzerland

Helicopter landing on the Jungfrau glacier — the Alps in silence above the clouds.

  • Eiger, Mönch & Jungfrau — the triple-summit view
  • Aletsch Glacier — Europe's longest from above
  • Private ski descents from glacier landing

Best: Dec – Apr (snow); Jun – Sep (clear skies)

🇦🇺

Great Barrier Reef Seaplane

Queensland, Australia

The only living structure visible from space — a 2,300km mosaic of coral from above.

  • Heart Reef — natural coral heart, only viewable by air
  • Whitehaven Beach — 7km of pure silica sand from above
  • Seaplane water landing in the outer reef

Best: Jun – Oct

🇧🇷

Amazon Rainforest Flight

Brazil & Peru

An infinite green ocean — the lungs of the planet viewed from a light aircraft.

  • Meeting of the Waters — Rio Negro meets Amazon from above
  • Flooded forest canopy at high water season
  • Remote lodge access only possible by small aircraft

Best: Jun – Nov

🇮🇸

Iceland Northern Lights Flight

Iceland

Chase the aurora from above the clouds — guaranteed clear-sky viewing by air.

  • Small aircraft fly above cloud cover to find the aurora
  • Volcanic landscape below, celestial curtains above
  • Combination: glacier overflight by day, aurora by night

Best: Sep – Mar

🇮🇹

Dolomites Helicopter

South Tyrol, Italy

Three Peaks, rose-lit at sunset — the most theatrical mountain range in Europe.

  • Tre Cime di Lavaredo — three towers from 500m above
  • Cortina d'Ampezzo amphitheatre from the air
  • Glacier landing on Marmolada — highest peak of the Dolomites

Best: Jun – Sep

🇨🇳

Zhangjiajie Aerial

Hunan Province, China

The Avatar mountains — 3,000 sandstone pillars rising through cloud from above.

  • World's longest cable car with glass-bottom cabins
  • Tianmen Mountain — "Heaven's Gate" arch in the cliff
  • Pillar formations reaching 200m from a light aircraft

Best: Apr – Oct

🇿🇲

Victoria Falls Flight

Zambia & Zimbabwe

The smoke that thunders — the world's largest waterfall sheet from a microlight.

  • 1.7km wide curtain of water visible for 50km away
  • Microlight flight straight over the falls in the mist
  • Helicopter sunset tour over the Zambezi floodplains

Best: Mar – May (full flow)

🇵🇪

Nazca Lines Flight

Ica Desert, Peru

2,000-year-old geoglyphs only visible from the air — one of Earth's great mysteries.

  • The Hummingbird, Spider, Monkey — giant earth drawings
  • 50km of lines in the coastal Peruvian desert
  • Small Cessna flight at low altitude over the figures

Best: May – Nov

🇬🇱

Greenland Ice Sheet

Greenland

The world's second largest ice sheet — a white continent seen from a Twin Otter.

  • Ilulissat Icefjord — UNESCO glacier calving into the sea
  • Dog-sled camp landing on the ice cap at 2,500m
  • Musk ox and Arctic fox spotted from low altitude

Best: Mar – Apr, Jun – Aug

🇦🇷

Patagonia — Torres del Paine

Chilean Patagonia

The Towers, the Grey Glacier, the Southern Ice Field — one flight, an eternity.

  • Torres del Paine granite spires from a helicopter
  • Perito Moreno Glacier — 60m ice wall calving below
  • Condors riding thermals at eye level

Best: Nov – Mar
