UNIT 1
Algebraic Foundations & Number Systems
Days 1–3 | June 22–24 • TEKS: A.11(A), A.11(B), A.1(C), A.1(E), A.10(A), A.10(B)
Assessment
20 min | 15 questions
Open Test ↗
Essential Question Why do the properties and structure of real numbers govern every algebraic step we take?
Day 1 Mon Jun 22 Real Number System & Properties A.11(A), A.11(B)
Objective: Classify real numbers; apply all five properties to justify algebraic steps.
🌸 Warm-Up
Number Sort: classify 10 number cards (√2, −3, ½, π, 0…) on a Venn diagram.
📖 Direct Instruction
Real number hierarchy. All five properties with numeric and algebraic examples.
✏️ Guided Practice
Partner activity – identify property justifying each simplification step.
🌟 Independent Practice
Classify 14 numbers; identify properties in 10 equations.
🎯 Exit Ticket
Classify {−√9, 2.5̄, π, 0, −8}; name property for 3(x+4)=3x+12.
📝 Homework
Khan Academy: 'Properties of numbers' + Kuta Worksheet #1
Day 2 Tue Jun 23 Order of Operations & Laws of Exponents A.1(C), A.11(B)
Objective: Apply PEMDAS; apply all laws of exponents (product, quotient, power, zero, negative).
🌸 Warm-Up
Error analysis: two different answers for 3+4²×2. What is 5⁰ and 2⁻³?
📖 Direct Instruction
PEMDAS nested examples. All exponent laws with algebraic verification.
✏️ Guided Practice
8 whiteboard problems alternating order-of-ops and exponent-law.
🌟 Independent Practice
Evaluate 6 expressions; simplify 12 exponential expressions.
🎯 Exit Ticket
Simplify (3x²y)(4xy³) and (2x³)²/x⁴. Evaluate 2a²−3b for a=−2, b=1.
📝 Homework
Khan Academy: 'Exponent properties' + Kuta Worksheet #2
Day 3 Wed Jun 24 Polynomial Operations + Unit 1 Quiz A.10(A), A.10(B)
Objective: Add, subtract, multiply polynomials; factor GCF; multiply binomials (FOIL).
🌸 Warm-Up
Combine: (3x²+2x−5)+(x²−4x+7) on whiteboards.
📖 Direct Instruction
Poly vocab → add/subtract → FOIL → area model → GCF factor.
✏️ Guided Practice
2 add, 2 subtract, 3 multiply, 3 GCF factor problems.
🌟 Independent Practice
6 add/sub, 8 multiply, 6 GCF factor → then Unit 1 Quiz (15 Q, 20 min).
🎯 Exit Ticket
Simplify (2x+3)(x−4) and factor 6x³−9x²+3x. [Quiz follows]
📝 Homework
Khan Academy: 'Polynomials' + Kuta Worksheet #3

UNIT 2
Linear Functions, Equations & Inequalities
Days 4–10 | June 25 – July 3 • TEKS: A.2(A–I), A.3(A–E), A.12(A)
Assessment
40 min | 25 questions
Open Test ↗
Essential Question How can we represent, interpret, and compare linear relationships using multiple forms?
Day 4 Thu Jun 25 Relations, Functions, Domain & Range A.2(A), A.12(A)
Objective: Determine function vs. relation; identify domain and range; use f(x) notation.
🌸 Warm-Up
4 mapping diagrams – vote function/not with thumbs.
📖 Direct Instruction
Define function (each input→one output). VLT. f(x) notation. Domain/range.
✏️ Guided Practice
Classify 6 representations; evaluate f(3), f(−1).
🌟 Independent Practice
12 problems – identify, domain/range, evaluate f(x).
🎯 Exit Ticket
Domain/range from graph. Is it a function? Evaluate f(2) if f(x)=3x−1.
📝 Homework
Khan Academy: 'Evaluating functions' + 'Domain and range'
Day 5 Fri Jun 26 Slope, Rate of Change & Arithmetic Sequences A.3(A), A.3(B)
Objective: Calculate slope from all representations; write arithmetic sequence formulas.
🌸 Warm-Up
Real-world graphs: describe slope meaning for 4 scenarios.
📖 Direct Instruction
m=(y₂−y₁)/(x₂−x₁). All four slope types. Arithmetic sequences (aₙ=a₁+(n−1)d).
✏️ Guided Practice
Slope from tables, graphs, two points; 3 sequence formulas.
🌟 Independent Practice
14 slope + 6 arithmetic sequence problems.
🎯 Exit Ticket
Slope through (−2,5) and (4,−1). Is 3,7,11,15… arithmetic? Write formula.
📝 Homework
Khan Academy: 'Slope' + 'Arithmetic sequences'
Day 6 Mon Jun 29 Slope-Intercept Form: Graph & Write A.2(B), A.3(C)
Objective: Write and graph y=mx+b; find x-intercepts; graph horizontal and vertical lines.
🌸 Warm-Up
Gallery walk: 4 posted graphs – record slope and y-intercept.
📖 Direct Instruction
y=mx+b: plot b, use slope. Horizontal y=k, vertical x=h. Find x-intercept (y=0).
✏️ Guided Practice
Graph y=2x−3, y=−½x+4, x=3, y=−2; write 3 equations from graphs.
🌟 Independent Practice
Write 6 equations; graph 6 functions.
🎯 Exit Ticket
Write equation from given graph. Find its x-intercept.
📝 Homework
Khan Academy: 'Slope-intercept form' + Kuta graphing worksheet
Day 7 Tue Jun 30 Standard Form & Point-Slope Form A.2(B), A.2(C)
Objective: Write/interpret Ax+By=C and y−y₁=m(x−x₁); convert among all three forms.
🌸 Warm-Up
Name the form: y=3x−2 / 2x+3y=6 / y−4=½(x+1). Which graphs fastest?
📖 Direct Instruction
Standard form via intercepts. Point-slope from a point + slope. Convert all three.
✏️ Guided Practice
Convert same equation through all three forms; graph from standard form.
🌟 Independent Practice
Convert between forms – 15 problems; write in all three forms.
🎯 Exit Ticket
Convert 4x−2y=10 to slope-intercept. Write point-slope for (3,−1), slope 2.
📝 Homework
Khan Academy: 'Point-slope form' + 'Standard form'
Day 8 Wed Jul 1 Writing Equations + Parallel & Perpendicular Lines A.2(C), A.3(E)
Objective: Write equations from two points/table/graph; write parallel (same slope) and perpendicular (neg. reciprocal) equations.
🌸 Warm-Up
Which slope pairs are parallel? Perpendicular? {2&2, 3&−1/3, ½&−2}
📖 Direct Instruction
Step-by-step equation writing. Parallel=same slope; perpendicular=neg. reciprocal.
✏️ Guided Practice
Write 3 equations; 2 parallel + 2 perpendicular lines.
🌟 Independent Practice
12 equation-writing + 6 parallel/perpendicular problems.
🎯 Exit Ticket
Write line through (2,−3) (a) parallel to y=4x+1 (b) perpendicular to it.
📝 Homework
Khan Academy: 'Writing equations' + 'Parallel & perpendicular'
Day 9 Thu Jul 2 Inequalities in 2 Variables & Direct Variation A.2(D), A.2(H)
Objective: Graph linear inequalities in two variables (solid/dashed, half-plane); write y=kx direct variation.
🌸 Warm-Up
Is (1,4) a solution to y>2x+1? Is (3,7)? What changes when you shade?
📖 Direct Instruction
Inequalities: boundary line → test point → shade. Direct variation y=kx, k=y/x.
✏️ Guided Practice
Graph 4 inequalities; write 2 from graphs; find k from 3 tables.
🌟 Independent Practice
Graph 6 inequalities; write 4 from graphs; 6 direct variation.
🎯 Exit Ticket
Graph y≤−2x+4. Write direct variation if y=15 when x=3.
📝 Homework
Khan Academy: 'Two-variable inequalities' + 'Direct variation'

Objective: Demonstrate mastery of all Unit 2 concepts.

Lightning round: rapid-fire whiteboard – all Unit 2 topics.

Review of most-missed exit ticket items. Visual anchor chart of all linear forms.

Carousel: 5 stations × 5 min (domain/range, slope, writing, ∥⊥, inequalities).
