Road · Scenic Drives Worldwide

The world's most legendary roads

🇺🇸

Route 66

Illinois → California, USA

"The Mother Road" — America's most iconic highway across eight states.

  • 2,400 miles of Americana, diners & desert
  • Cadillac Ranch, Petrified Forest, Mojave Desert
  • Chicago to Santa Monica — coast to coast

Best: Apr – Jun, Sep – Oct

🇮🇹

Amalfi Coast Drive

Salerno to Sorrento, Italy

Hairpin bends above sheer cliffs, coloured villages clinging to limestone.

  • Positano, Ravello, Praiano — pastel perched towns
  • SS163 — 30km of clifftop Mediterranean drama
  • UNESCO World Heritage coastline

Best: May – Jun, Sep

🇳🇴

Norwegian Atlantic Road

Kristiansund, Norway

Eight bridges over open sea — voted the world's most beautiful detour.

  • Storseisundet Bridge — arches over crashing waves
  • Whale watching and Arctic light
  • Connects islands through wild North Atlantic

Best: Jun – Aug

🇦🇺

Great Ocean Road

Victoria, Australia

Rainforest meets Southern Ocean — built by returned soldiers as a memorial.

  • Twelve Apostles — limestone stacks rising from the sea
  • Otway Rainforest and koala spotting
  • 243km of dramatic coastal scenery

Best: Oct – Apr

🇷🇴

Transfăgărășan Highway

Carpathian Mountains, Romania

Top Gear called it the world's best road — 90km of mountain switchbacks.

  • Peaks above 2,000m through Fagaras Mountains
  • Bâlea Lake — glacial lake at the summit
  • Open only June – October due to snowfall

Best: Jul – Sep

🇺🇸

Pacific Coast Highway

California, USA

Highway 1 — cliffs, redwoods and surf culture along the California dream.

  • Big Sur — the most dramatic stretch in America
  • Bixby Creek Bridge, McWay Falls
  • San Francisco to Los Angeles — 650 miles

Best: Mar – May, Sep – Nov

🇵🇰

Karakoram Highway

Pakistan & China

The world's highest paved international road — 8th wonder of the world.

  • Passes three of the world's mightiest mountain ranges
  • Attabad Lake — turquoise glacial water
  • Khunjerab Pass at 4,693m altitude

Best: May – Oct

🇮🇸

Ring Road · Route 1

Iceland

Circumnavigate an entire island — volcanoes, glaciers, geysers and fjords.

  • 1,332km encircling the full island
  • Skaftafell glacier, Jökulsárlón lagoon, Geysir
  • Northern Lights above the road in winter

Best: Jun – Aug (summer); Nov – Feb (lights)

🇿🇦

Garden Route

Western Cape, South Africa

Forest, lagoon, cliff and coast — South Africa's most celebrated road trip.

  • Knysna Heads, Tsitsikamma Forest, Plettenberg Bay
  • Whale watching at Hermanus
  • 800km from Cape Town to Port Elizabeth

Best: Sep – Apr

🇿🇦

Chapman's Peak Drive

Cape Peninsula, South Africa

Nine kilometres of cliff-carved road between mountain and Atlantic — sheer drama.

  • 114 curves blasted from solid granite
  • Hout Bay panorama and Cape fur seals below
  • Sunset from the peak lookout — unmissable

Best: Oct – Apr

🇺🇸

Hana Highway

Maui, Hawaii, USA

620 curves, 59 bridges, one-lane passes — Hawaii's most hypnotic coastal drive.

  • Twin Falls, Wai'anapanapa Black Sand Beach
  • Road to Hana past bamboo forests and sea cliffs
  • Seven Sacred Pools at journey's end

Best: Apr – Sep

🇨🇱

Carretera Austral

Patagonia, Chile

1,240km of unpaved Patagonian wilderness — glaciers, volcanoes and zero crowds.

  • Queulat Hanging Glacier, Marble Caves
  • Coyhaique to Villa O'Higgins — true end of the road
  • Condors circling above ferries crossing fjords

Best: Nov – Mar

🇮🇹

Stelvio Pass

South Tyrol, Italy

48 hairpin bends to 2,757m — the Everest of mountain driving.

  • Highest paved mountain pass in the Eastern Alps
  • Ortler glacier visible from the summit
  • Open only Jun – Sep when snow clears

Best: Jun – Sep

🇳🇿

Milford Road

Fiordland, New Zealand

Through the Homer Tunnel into a world of waterfalls and mirror-black fjord.

  • The Divide, Monkey Creek, Mirror Lakes
  • Homer Tunnel — hand-carved through solid rock
  • Chasm Walk at road's end beside Milford Sound

Best: Oct – Apr

🇲🇦

Dades Valley Road

High Atlas, Morocco

Kasbah road through Saharan gorges, rose valleys and Atlas foothills.

  • Aït Benhaddou UNESCO kasbah on the route
  • Gorges du Dadès — dramatic switchbacks above a canyon
  • Sahara access via Merzouga — dune sunrises

Best: Mar – May, Sep – Nov
