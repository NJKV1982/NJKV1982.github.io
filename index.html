What Is ROAM

Three paths.
One world.
Infinite journeys.

Most travel companies sell destinations. We sell the way you get there — because the road through Patagonia, the ocean crossing between islands, the view from 35,000 feet over the Himalayas — these are not just methods of transport. They are the journey itself.


ROAM is a bespoke travel consulting studio built around three modes: Road, Ocean, and Air. We combine them, sequence them, and tailor them entirely to you.

Road · Scenic Drives Worldwide

The world's most legendary roads

🇺🇸

Route 66

Illinois → California, USA

"The Mother Road" — America's most iconic highway across eight states.

  • 2,400 miles of Americana, diners & desert
  • Cadillac Ranch, Petrified Forest, Mojave Desert
  • Chicago to Santa Monica — coast to coast

Best: Apr – Jun, Sep – Oct

🇮🇹

Amalfi Coast Drive

Salerno to Sorrento, Italy

Hairpin bends above sheer cliffs, coloured villages clinging to limestone.

  • Positano, Ravello, Praiano — pastel perched towns
  • SS163 — 30km of clifftop Mediterranean drama
  • UNESCO World Heritage coastline

Best: May – Jun, Sep

🇳🇴

Norwegian Atlantic Road

Kristiansund, Norway

Eight bridges over open sea — voted the world's most beautiful detour.

  • Storseisundet Bridge — arches over crashing waves
  • Whale watching and Arctic light
  • Connects islands through wild North Atlantic

Best: Jun – Aug

🇦🇺

Great Ocean Road

Victoria, Australia

Rainforest meets Southern Ocean — built by returned soldiers as a memorial.

  • Twelve Apostles — limestone stacks rising from the sea
  • Otway Rainforest and koala spotting
  • 243km of dramatic coastal scenery

Best: Oct – Apr

🇷🇴

Transfăgărășan Highway

Carpathian Mountains, Romania

Top Gear called it the world's best road — 90km of mountain switchbacks.

  • Peaks above 2,000m through Fagaras Mountains
  • Bâlea Lake — glacial lake at the summit
  • Open only June – October due to snowfall

Best: Jul – Sep

🇺🇸

Pacific Coast Highway

California, USA

Highway 1 — cliffs, redwoods and surf culture along the California dream.

  • Big Sur — the most dramatic stretch in America
  • Bixby Creek Bridge, McWay Falls
  • San Francisco to Los Angeles — 650 miles

Best: Mar – May, Sep – Nov

🇵🇰

Karakoram Highway

Pakistan & China

The world's highest paved international road — 8th wonder of the world.

  • Passes three of the world's mightiest mountain ranges
  • Attabad Lake — turquoise glacial water
  • Khunjerab Pass at 4,693m altitude

Best: May – Oct

🇮🇸

Ring Road · Route 1

Iceland

Circumnavigate an entire island — volcanoes, glaciers, geysers and fjords.

  • 1,332km encircling the full island
  • Skaftafell glacier, Jökulsárlón lagoon, Geysir
  • Northern Lights above the road in winter

Best: Jun – Aug (summer); Nov – Feb (lights)

🇿🇦

Garden Route

Western Cape, South Africa

Forest, lagoon, cliff and coast — South Africa's most celebrated road trip.

  • Knysna Heads, Tsitsikamma Forest, Plettenberg Bay
  • Whale watching at Hermanus
  • 800km from Cape Town to Port Elizabeth

Best: Sep – Apr

🇿🇦

Chapman's Peak Drive

Cape Peninsula, South Africa

Nine kilometres of cliff-carved road between mountain and Atlantic — sheer drama.

  • 114 curves blasted from solid granite
  • Hout Bay panorama and Cape fur seals below
  • Sunset from the peak lookout — unmissable

Best: Oct – Apr

🇺🇸

Hana Highway

Maui, Hawaii, USA

620 curves, 59 bridges, one-lane passes — Hawaii's most hypnotic coastal drive.

  • Twin Falls, Wai'anapanapa Black Sand Beach
  • Road to Hana past bamboo forests and sea cliffs
  • Seven Sacred Pools at journey's end

Best: Apr – Sep

🇨🇱

Carretera Austral

Patagonia, Chile

1,240km of unpaved Patagonian wilderness — glaciers, volcanoes and zero crowds.

  • Queulat Hanging Glacier, Marble Caves
  • Coyhaique to Villa O'Higgins — true end of the road
  • Condors circling above ferries crossing fjords

Best: Nov – Mar

🇮🇹

Stelvio Pass

South Tyrol, Italy

48 hairpin bends to 2,757m — the Everest of mountain driving.

  • Highest paved mountain pass in the Eastern Alps
  • Ortler glacier visible from the summit
  • Open only Jun – Sep when snow clears

Best: Jun – Sep

🇳🇿

Milford Road

Fiordland, New Zealand

Through the Homer Tunnel into a world of waterfalls and mirror-black fjord.

  • The Divide, Monkey Creek, Mirror Lakes
  • Homer Tunnel — hand-carved through solid rock
  • Chasm Walk at road's end beside Milford Sound

Best: Oct – Apr

🇲🇦

Dades Valley Road

High Atlas, Morocco

Kasbah road through Saharan gorges, rose valleys and Atlas foothills.

  • Aït Benhaddou UNESCO kasbah on the route
  • Gorges du Dadès — dramatic switchbacks above a canyon
  • Sahara access via Merzouga — dune sunrises

Best: Mar – May, Sep – Nov

Ocean · Islands · Cruises Worldwide

Where the sea takes you somewhere extraordinary

🇲🇻

Maldives

Indian Ocean

1,200 coral islands — overwater bungalows above the clearest water on Earth.

  • Bioluminescent plankton beaches at night
  • Manta ray & whale shark snorkelling
  • Private island resorts accessible only by seaplane

Best: Nov – Apr

🇬🇷

Greek Cyclades

Aegean Sea, Greece

Sailing between white villages, ancient ruins and volcanic caldera views.

  • Santorini caldera — the world's most dramatic harbour
  • Mykonos, Naxos, Paros, Milos — each unique
  • Ancient Delos — mythological island sanctuary

Best: May – Jun, Sep – Oct

🇳🇴

Norwegian Fjords Cruise

Western Norway

Sail into glacier-carved walls 1,000m high — one of Earth's greatest spectacles.

  • Geirangerfjord & Nærøyfjord — dual UNESCO sites
  • Waterfalls dropping directly into the sea
  • Village stops: Flåm, Ålesund, Bergen

Best: May – Sep

🇦🇶

Antarctica

Southern Ocean

The last great wilderness — penguin colonies, tabular icebergs, polar silence.

  • Drake Passage crossing — the world's roughest sea
  • Zodiac landings among emperor penguin colonies
  • Humpback whale feeding in icy bays

Best: Nov – Mar

🇪🇨

Galápagos Islands

Pacific Ocean, Ecuador

Darwin's living laboratory — wildlife that shows no fear of humans.

  • Giant tortoises, marine iguanas, blue-footed boobies
  • Snorkelling with sea lions and hammerhead sharks
  • Each island a distinct ecosystem

Best: Jun – Nov

🇸🇨

Seychelles

Indian Ocean

115 granite islands — prehistoric boulders, aldabra tortoises, pure isolation.

  • Anse Source d'Argent — world's most photographed beach
  • Vallée de Mai — ancient coco de mer palm forest
  • Private island resorts: North, Fregate, Desroches

Best: Apr – May, Oct – Nov

🇻🇳

Ha Long Bay

Gulf of Tonkin, Vietnam

2,000 limestone karsts rising from emerald sea — a UNESCO masterpiece.

  • Junk boat overnight cruise through the karsts
  • Kayaking into hidden caves and lagoons
  • Fishing villages floating between the islands

Best: Oct – Apr

🇮🇩

Raja Ampat

West Papua, Indonesia

The world's richest marine biodiversity — 75% of all known coral species.

  • Misool, Waigeo, Batanta — four main island groups
  • Manta rays gliding through crystal channels
  • Mushroom-shaped karst islands above the surface

Best: Oct – Apr

🇵🇫

Bora Bora & French Polynesia

South Pacific

The romanticised lagoon island — turquoise atoll with Mount Otemanu at its heart.

  • Overwater bungalows above a coral garden
  • Lagoon safari — sharks, rays, turtles
  • Island hop: Moorea, Huahine, Tikehau

Best: May – Oct

🇵🇭

Palawan

Philippines

An island of islands — limestone cliffs, hidden lagoons and underground rivers.

  • El Nido — kayak through cathedral-like karst lagoons
  • Puerto Princesa Underground River — UNESCO cave wonder
  • Coron — wreck diving among WWII Japanese fleet

Best: Nov – May

🇵🇹

Azores

Mid-Atlantic, Portugal

Nine volcanic islands mid-Atlantic — whale watching capital of the world.

  • Sete Cidades twin crater lakes on São Miguel
  • Sperm whale, blue whale and dolphin encounters
  • Thermal pools bubbling into the ocean at Furnas

Best: May – Sep

🇦🇺

Whitsundays

Queensland, Australia

74 islands in the Coral Sea — Whitehaven Beach is silica so pure it squeaks.

  • Whitehaven Beach — consistently voted world's best
  • Sailing between uninhabited islands
  • Heart Reef in the Great Barrier Reef above

Best: Jun – Oct

🇳🇴

Lofoten Islands

Arctic Norway

Dramatic Arctic peaks rising from the sea — fishing villages painted blood-red.

  • Midnight sun from June – July with full daylight
  • Northern Lights reflected in perfect fjord water
  • Reine — consistently ranked world's most scenic village

Best: Jun – Aug (sun); Dec – Feb (lights)

🇨🇷

Caribbean Coast

Caribbean Sea

700 islands, 26 languages, one endless summer — from jungle to turquoise.

  • St Lucia — volcanic Pitons above yacht-filled bay
  • Belize Barrier Reef — second largest in the world
  • BVI, Turks & Caicos, Grenada — each a world apart

Best: Dec – Apr

🇯🇵

Okinawa Islands

East China Sea, Japan

Japan's tropical secret — mangroves, coral gardens and ancient Ryukyu culture.

  • Kerama Islands — clearest water in Japan (Kerama Blue)
  • Iriomote — jungle river kayaking in a near-wilderness
  • Whale shark diving at Yonaguni, Japan's westernmost island

Best: Apr – Jun, Sep – Nov

Air · Scenic Flights · Aerial Wonders

Views the Earth keeps for those who fly

🇹🇷

Cappadocia Balloon Flight

Göreme, Turkey

Sunrise over a lunar landscape of fairy chimneys — the world's finest balloon flight.

  • 100+ balloons rising together at dawn
  • Rock-carved churches and underground cities below
  • Champagne landing in a vineyard after 1hr in the air

Best: Apr – Jun, Sep – Nov

🇺🇸

Grand Canyon Helicopter

Arizona, USA

Descend 1,500m below the rim — the canyon only truly reveals itself from above.

  • West Rim, South Rim & North Rim routes
  • Land on the canyon floor beside the Colorado River
  • Dawn flights when the light turns the rock to fire

Best: Mar – May, Sep – Nov

🇹🇿

Serengeti Balloon Safari

Tanzania

Float above the Great Migration — millions of wildebeest from 300 metres up.

  • Lions, elephants and cheetah from the air at dawn
  • Mara River crossing visible from above
  • Full bush breakfast on landing in the plains

Best: Jul – Oct (migration peak)

🇳🇿

Milford Sound Scenic Flight

Fiordland, New Zealand

Fly over the eighth wonder — glaciers, waterfalls and black fjord from a floatplane.

  • Mitre Peak — 1,692m straight out of the sea
  • Doubtful Sound and Dusky Sound from above
  • Glacier neve landings by ski-plane

Best: Oct – Apr

🇳🇦

Namib Desert Aerial

Namibia

The world's oldest desert from above — star dunes 300m high meeting the Atlantic.

  • Sossusvlei dunes — apricot and ochre from the air
  • Skeleton Coast — shipwrecks in the sand from 500m
  • Fish River Canyon — Africa's Grand Canyon

Best: May – Sep

🇨🇭

Swiss Alps Glacier Flight

Switzerland

Helicopter landing on the Jungfrau glacier — the Alps in silence above the clouds.

  • Eiger, Mönch & Jungfrau — the triple-summit view
  • Aletsch Glacier — Europe's longest from above
  • Private ski descents from glacier landing

Best: Dec – Apr (snow); Jun – Sep (clear skies)

🇦🇺

Great Barrier Reef Seaplane

Queensland, Australia

The only living structure visible from space — a 2,300km mosaic of coral from above.

  • Heart Reef — natural coral heart, only viewable by air
  • Whitehaven Beach — 7km of pure silica sand from above
  • Seaplane water landing in the outer reef

Best: Jun – Oct

🇧🇷

Amazon Rainforest Flight

Brazil & Peru

An infinite green ocean — the lungs of the planet viewed from a light aircraft.

  • Meeting of the Waters — Rio Negro meets Amazon from above
  • Flooded forest canopy at high water season
  • Remote lodge access only possible by small aircraft

Best: Jun – Nov

🇮🇸

Iceland Northern Lights Flight

Iceland

Chase the aurora from above the clouds — guaranteed clear-sky viewing by air.

  • Small aircraft fly above cloud cover to find the aurora
  • Volcanic landscape below, celestial curtains above
  • Combination: glacier overflight by day, aurora by night

Best: Sep – Mar

🇮🇹

Dolomites Helicopter

South Tyrol, Italy

Three Peaks, rose-lit at sunset — the most theatrical mountain range in Europe.

  • Tre Cime di Lavaredo — three towers from 500m above
  • Cortina d'Ampezzo amphitheatre from the air
  • Glacier landing on Marmolada — highest peak of the Dolomites

Best: Jun – Sep

🇨🇳

Zhangjiajie Aerial

Hunan Province, China

The Avatar mountains — 3,000 sandstone pillars rising through cloud from above.

  • World's longest cable car with glass-bottom cabins
  • Tianmen Mountain — "Heaven's Gate" arch in the cliff
  • Pillar formations reaching 200m from a light aircraft

Best: Apr – Oct

🇿🇲

Victoria Falls Flight

Zambia & Zimbabwe

The smoke that thunders — the world's largest waterfall sheet from a microlight.

  • 1.7km wide curtain of water visible for 50km away
  • Microlight flight straight over the falls in the mist
  • Helicopter sunset tour over the Zambezi floodplains

Best: Mar – May (full flow)

🇵🇪

Nazca Lines Flight

Ica Desert, Peru

2,000-year-old geoglyphs only visible from the air — one of Earth's great mysteries.

  • The Hummingbird, Spider, Monkey — giant earth drawings
  • 50km of lines in the coastal Peruvian desert
  • Small Cessna flight at low altitude over the figures

Best: May – Nov

🇬🇱

Greenland Ice Sheet

Greenland

The world's second largest ice sheet — a white continent seen from a Twin Otter.

  • Ilulissat Icefjord — UNESCO glacier calving into the sea
  • Dog-sled camp landing on the ice cap at 2,500m
  • Musk ox and Arctic fox spotted from low altitude

Best: Mar – Apr, Jun – Aug

🇦🇷

Patagonia — Torres del Paine

Chilean Patagonia

The Towers, the Grey Glacier, the Southern Ice Field — one flight, an eternity.

  • Torres del Paine granite spires from a helicopter
  • Perito Moreno Glacier — 60m ice wall calving below
  • Condors riding thermals at eye level

Best: Nov – Mar
