What Is ROAM

Three paths.
One world.
Infinite journeys.

Most travel companies sell destinations. We sell the way you get there — because the road through Patagonia, the ocean crossing between islands, the view from 35,000 feet over the Himalayas — these are not just methods of transport. They are the journey itself.


ROAM is a bespoke travel consulting studio built around three modes: Road, Ocean, and Air. We combine them, sequence them, and tailor them entirely to you.

01 · Road
🛣️

The road doesn't just
take you there.
It shapes you.

Highway · Mountain Pass · Desert Trail · Coastal Drive

There is a particular kind of freedom that only the road delivers. The ability to stop when something catches your eye. To take the long way. To watch the landscape change through a windscreen as hours blur into discovery. We design road journeys that honour this — self-drives, chauffeured expeditions and guided overland crossings across the world's most dramatic terrain.

The Founder

Janki Vyas

Founder · ROAM Travel Consulting

"I've driven through the Namib Desert alone at sunrise, sailed across the Adriatic in a storm, and watched the Alps pass below at 35,000 feet thinking — this is the same Earth. Road, ocean, air. They each show you something different about it."

ROAM was born from a simple observation: the way you travel changes what you discover. After a decade of building journeys for clients across every mode of movement, I decided to build a company that took that seriously — one that treats the road, the ocean and the sky not as logistics, but as experiences in themselves.
