Ocean · Islands · Cruises Worldwide

Where the sea takes you somewhere extraordinary

🇲🇻

Maldives

Indian Ocean

1,200 coral islands — overwater bungalows above the clearest water on Earth.

  • Bioluminescent plankton beaches at night
  • Manta ray & whale shark snorkelling
  • Private island resorts accessible only by seaplane

Best: Nov – Apr

🇬🇷

Greek Cyclades

Aegean Sea, Greece

Sailing between white villages, ancient ruins and volcanic caldera views.

  • Santorini caldera — the world's most dramatic harbour
  • Mykonos, Naxos, Paros, Milos — each unique
  • Ancient Delos — mythological island sanctuary

Best: May – Jun, Sep – Oct

🇳🇴

Norwegian Fjords Cruise

Western Norway

Sail into glacier-carved walls 1,000m high — one of Earth's greatest spectacles.

  • Geirangerfjord & Nærøyfjord — dual UNESCO sites
  • Waterfalls dropping directly into the sea
  • Village stops: Flåm, Ålesund, Bergen

Best: May – Sep

🇦🇶

Antarctica

Southern Ocean

The last great wilderness — penguin colonies, tabular icebergs, polar silence.

  • Drake Passage crossing — the world's roughest sea
  • Zodiac landings among emperor penguin colonies
  • Humpback whale feeding in icy bays

Best: Nov – Mar

🇪🇨

Galápagos Islands

Pacific Ocean, Ecuador

Darwin's living laboratory — wildlife that shows no fear of humans.

  • Giant tortoises, marine iguanas, blue-footed boobies
  • Snorkelling with sea lions and hammerhead sharks
  • Each island a distinct ecosystem

Best: Jun – Nov

🇸🇨

Seychelles

Indian Ocean

115 granite islands — prehistoric boulders, aldabra tortoises, pure isolation.

  • Anse Source d'Argent — world's most photographed beach
  • Vallée de Mai — ancient coco de mer palm forest
  • Private island resorts: North, Fregate, Desroches

Best: Apr – May, Oct – Nov

🇻🇳

Ha Long Bay

Gulf of Tonkin, Vietnam

2,000 limestone karsts rising from emerald sea — a UNESCO masterpiece.

  • Junk boat overnight cruise through the karsts
  • Kayaking into hidden caves and lagoons
  • Fishing villages floating between the islands

Best: Oct – Apr

🇮🇩

Raja Ampat

West Papua, Indonesia

The world's richest marine biodiversity — 75% of all known coral species.

  • Misool, Waigeo, Batanta — four main island groups
  • Manta rays gliding through crystal channels
  • Mushroom-shaped karst islands above the surface

Best: Oct – Apr

🇵🇫

Bora Bora & French Polynesia

South Pacific

The romanticised lagoon island — turquoise atoll with Mount Otemanu at its heart.

  • Overwater bungalows above a coral garden
  • Lagoon safari — sharks, rays, turtles
  • Island hop: Moorea, Huahine, Tikehau

Best: May – Oct

🇵🇭

Palawan

Philippines

An island of islands — limestone cliffs, hidden lagoons and underground rivers.

  • El Nido — kayak through cathedral-like karst lagoons
  • Puerto Princesa Underground River — UNESCO cave wonder
  • Coron — wreck diving among WWII Japanese fleet

Best: Nov – May

🇵🇹

Azores

Mid-Atlantic, Portugal

Nine volcanic islands mid-Atlantic — whale watching capital of the world.

  • Sete Cidades twin crater lakes on São Miguel
  • Sperm whale, blue whale and dolphin encounters
  • Thermal pools bubbling into the ocean at Furnas

Best: May – Sep

🇦🇺

Whitsundays

Queensland, Australia

74 islands in the Coral Sea — Whitehaven Beach is silica so pure it squeaks.

  • Whitehaven Beach — consistently voted world's best
  • Sailing between uninhabited islands
  • Heart Reef in the Great Barrier Reef above

Best: Jun – Oct

🇳🇴

Lofoten Islands

Arctic Norway

Dramatic Arctic peaks rising from the sea — fishing villages painted blood-red.

  • Midnight sun from June – July with full daylight
  • Northern Lights reflected in perfect fjord water
  • Reine — consistently ranked world's most scenic village

Best: Jun – Aug (sun); Dec – Feb (lights)

🇨🇷

Caribbean Islands

Caribbean Sea

700 islands, 26 languages, one endless summer — from jungle to turquoise.

  • St Lucia — volcanic Pitons above yacht-filled bay
  • Belize Barrier Reef — second largest in the world
  • BVI, Turks & Caicos, Grenada — each a world apart

Best: Dec – Apr

🇯🇵

Okinawa Islands

East China Sea, Japan

Japan's tropical secret — mangroves, coral gardens and ancient Ryukyu culture.

  • Kerama Islands — clearest water in Japan (Kerama Blue)
  • Iriomote — jungle river kayaking in a near-wilderness
  • Whale shark diving at Yonaguni, Japan's westernmost island

Best: Apr – Jun, Sep – Nov
